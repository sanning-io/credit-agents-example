Nordvik Haulage AB

Reg. no. 556-XXXX (fictional). Annual Report, financial year 1 October 2024 to 30 September 2025.

Administration report

Nordvik Haulage AB operates nine tractor units on long-haul routes between southern and central Sweden, carrying general cargo and temperature-controlled freight for industrial and retail customers. The board's principal activity statement is unchanged from the prior year.

The financial year fell during a sustained freight rate downturn across the Swedish long-haul segment. Spot rates softened through the winter and did not recover to prior levels until after the year end. The board notes that the fleet ran at reduced utilization for parts of the year as a result, which is reflected in the year's turnover and operating margin. No tractor units were added or disposed of during the year. In March 2025 a major customer, a food wholesaler, went bankrupt owing SEK 400,000, written off in full as a bad debt within operating expenses. In the previous financial year the company wrote off SEK 150,000 owed by two smaller customers. A new contract replacing the work began in October 2025, for an initial term of twelve months.
