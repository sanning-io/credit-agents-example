Bruksvik Finans AB

Commercial vehicle credit policy: hire purchase, transport and logistics segment. Version 3.3, effective 1 July 2026.

1. Purpose and scope

This policy sets the minimum underwriting standard for hire purchase financing of new and used commercial vehicles to transport and logistics operators. It applies to every application reviewed by an underwriter and confirmed by a checker before an offer issues.

2. Debt service coverage ratio

The applicant's debt service coverage ratio (DSCR) is the primary affordability test.

DSCR = EBITDA / total annual debt service.

Total annual debt service is the applicant's existing debt service plus the annual debt service of the facility applied for, calculated on the proposed term and rate.

The minimum DSCR for approval is 1.25x.

3. Assessment window

DSCR is measured over the trailing 24 months of trading history where 24 months of trading history is available to the applicant.

Where the applicant has supplied fewer than 24 months of statements, the underwriter requests the applicant's most recent annual report, or equivalent filed accounts, to complete the window. No period inside the window is left out.

Where 24 months of history shows seasonality, the underwriter notes the pattern in the assessment; seasonality on its own is not grounds to exclude a weaker period from the window.

4. Adjusted earnings

Where the underwriter judges that an item or a period inside the window does not reflect the applicant's ongoing earnings and will not recur, it may also calculate DSCR on adjusted earnings. For each adjustment the underwriter states the amount, the period it applies to and the evidence for it. It always shows DSCR on unadjusted earnings beside the adjusted figure.

An application may be approved on adjusted earnings only where the checker agrees with every adjustment. Otherwise sections 5 and 6 apply to DSCR on unadjusted earnings.

5. Deposit rules

Applicant DSCR (24-month window) Minimum deposit
1.25x or above 10% of the financed asset value
1.10x up to 1.25x 15% of the financed asset value
Below 1.10x Decline, or refer to a senior credit officer

A deposit reduces the amount financed and therefore the annual debt service of the facility. The underwriter recalculates DSCR on the reduced facility amount after applying a deposit; a deposit that still leaves the recalculated DSCR below 1.25x supports approval with conditions rather than approval outright.

6. Conditions available to an underwriter

Where the 24-month DSCR sits below 1.25x but at or above 1.10x after the applicable deposit, the underwriter may recommend approval with conditions rather than decline. Available conditions, used alone or together:

  • Deposit uplift. A deposit at or above the tier in section 5, reducing the facility amount and the resulting annual debt service.
  • Quarterly DSCR covenant. The applicant reports trailing 12-month EBITDA and debt service each quarter for the life of the facility; a breach is an event of review.
  • Phased release. Where the facility finances more than one vehicle, units beyond the first are released only once the applicant has demonstrated at least two consecutive quarters at or above the 1.25x minimum.
  • Personal or parent guarantee. Where ownership structure permits, a guarantee from a director or parent company.

An underwriter records which conditions were applied and why in the assessment. A checker reviewing the assessment confirms the conditions applied match what section 5 and this section permit for the DSCR and deposit shown.

7. The checker's review

Every assessment is reviewed by a checker who is independent of the underwriter. The checker reads the same documents, recalculates DSCR itself, and either agrees with the assessment or challenges it, stating its reasons. The underwriter answers a challenge once: it revises its assessment, or it maintains it and states why. The checker then concludes.

8. Escalation

An application that does not reach 1.25x on the 24-month window even after the maximum deposit and full use of section 6's conditions is declined, or referred to a senior credit officer.

Where the underwriter and the checker still disagree after the underwriter has answered a challenge, the checker refers the application to a senior credit officer with both views attached.
